01. Getting Started

1

Explore the Dashboard: View your level, current XP bar, gold coins count, and the main navigation panel on the left sidebar.

2

Rename Your Hero: Click directly on the default name Hero to rename your character. Hit Enter to save your new identity.

3

Customize Your Avatar: Click your avatar circle in the sidebar to open the custom avatar generator. Click repeatedly to roll randomized elements!

💪 Stat Tracking: Complete tasks to gain XP, Gold, and raise your character attributes (Strength, Intellect, Wealth, Health).

Marketing Soul Chapter 2

02. Quests & Dailies

1

Dailies vs Quests: Dailies are repetitive habits (e.g. gym, study) that reset every single day. Active Quests are one-off to-do tasks.

2

Create Missions: Click Add Daily or Add Quest. Set a title, select a difficulty rating (Trivial, Easy, Medium, Hard), and link a stat attribute.

3

Claim Loot: Click the checkmark to complete a task. You will hear an 8-bit chiptune sound and receive Gold, XP, and stat points instantly!

🔥 Streaks: Completing Dailies consecutively increases your streak badge multiplier. Skipping Dailies resets the streak!

03. Skill Tree & Titles

1

Stat Requirements: Each node in the Skill Tree (e.g. Acrobatics, Financial Freedom, Hydration Expert) requires a specific attribute score to unlock.

2

Spend Gold: Invest your hard-earned Gold coins to purchase and unlock skills. Unlocked nodes glow with neon animations.

3

Unlocking Titles: Unlocking skills grants passive title bonuses (e.g., Novice to Sage or Grandmaster) that reflect on your profile sidebar.

🧠 Progression: Focus on building balanced stats to unlock hybrid skills further down the skill paths.

04. The Reward Shop

1

Set Custom Rewards: Add real-life rewards (e.g. "Watch 1 episode of anime", "Eat ice cream") and assign them custom gold prices.

2

Earn Shop Stock: The shop also stocks automated consumable items like XP Boosters or Health Potions to assist your journey.

3

Spend Your Gold: Buy rewards using gold earned from quests. Buying triggers a satisfying shop sound and deducts gold.

🍦 Rule of Honor: Only indulge in your real-life rewards after you successfully buy them in the Reward Shop!

05. Notepad & Alarms

1

Create Sticky Notes: Write down checklists or thoughts, pick one of the pastel background color tags, and click Save Note.

2

Set Datetime Alarms: Specify a future date and time in the editor. Notes with set alarms display a glowing reminder badge.

3

Acknowledge Alarms: When the time arrives, a flashing neon alarm modal overlays the app, accompanied by retro synth tones. Tap **Acknowledge** to silence.

⏰ Focus Fix: Click any saved note card to automatically load its details into the editor and center focus on inputs for editing.

Marketing Soul Chapter 6

06. Settings & Syncing

1

Audio Customization: Toggle app sound effects on or off and adjust the global synthesizer volume using the interactive slider.

2

Save & Export: Click Export State to download your character progress, stats, shop items, and notepad items as a JSON text backup.

3

Import & Recovery: To play on another device, click Import State and paste your saved JSON data to instantly restore your adventure.

⚠️ Warning: The "Reset State" button permanently wipes your locally saved hero data. Back up before resetting!

07. Standalone App Setup

1

Progressive Web App (PWA): Marketing Soul features native PWA integration, allowing it to be installed as a standalone app on both mobile devices and laptops, running 100% offline.

2

On Laptops / Desktops: Open the app folder or URL in Chrome or Edge. Look for the "Install App" icon (🖥️+) in the right side of the address bar. Click install to add a standalone launcher to your desktop.

3

On Mobile Devices (Android & iOS): Load the app. On iOS Safari, tap the Share button and select "Add to Home Screen". On Android Chrome, tap the Menu (three dots) and select "Install App".

📱 Zero Latency: Running as an installed PWA hides browser toolbars, runs in full-screen window, and uses local service worker caching for instant loading.
